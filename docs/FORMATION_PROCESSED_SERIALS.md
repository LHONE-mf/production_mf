# Explicit processed-cell serials

Formation's existing `serialRange` remains the full input, even when `cellsOut` is smaller. It is never an inferred processed subset, final QC decision, finished-goods release or warehouse acceptance.

A separate immutable `FormationSerialIdentity` records the complete actual processed subset. It preserves the original saved Formation result and its Assembly/filling/walzing lineage, the original input prefix, explicit sorted ranges, exact processed count, declaring local author/role/time and evidence. It neither edits the original observation nor creates stock. No cathode/anode pairing or electrodes-per-cell rule is inferred.

Only the original active Formation author with the original role, or an active local Technologist/Master/Chief, can declare. The frozen declaration remains bound to its actual declaring actor and role; later role changes cannot silently rewrite it. A valid linked source and matching saved ancestors are required. Legacy unlinked observations remain preserved and ineligible for new identity declarations. One declaration per Formation result and Assembly-output identity prevents duplicate aliases. A different declaration cannot overwrite an earlier one.

Ranges must be explicit, nonnegative safe integers, within the full input, ascending and nonoverlapping, and total exactly `cellsOut`. Up to 1000 ranges; no serial list expansion or automatic allocation. For example, full input 10–13 with processed 2, scrap 1 and unknown 1 may explicitly declare 10 and 12; the app does not assign the other serials to scrap or unknown. A declaration is attribution from recorded evidence, not equipment verification.

Main Dexie **23** adds `formationSerialIdentities` without rewriting existing rows; inventory outbox stays **v7**, PostgreSQL stays **031**. Declaration and journal entry commit together, or neither is saved. Exact retries preserve the original declaration. Existing source records are not deleted, merged or rebased. Local-only archives from v21/v22 remain compatible; new archives retain identities and full original lineage. Device-bound warehouse claims and hybrid authority still cannot transfer through local-only restore.

Signed `formation-serial-identity` packets preserve the same immutable original record. They require matching declared sender/role and saved source lineage, correct stage and normal non-forced packet checks. A workshop group signature does not authenticate an individual, grant server rights, confirm QC or accept warehouse stock. A receiver must already have the original Formation result and required ancestor records. Original author attribution and original IDs survive exact reimport.

The Formation screen's **Номера обработанных** form uses the existing actor-scoped local draft store. Typed values persist offline. Explicit save freezes the original declaration and full source before the atomic journal write. A failed/unknown save retries that same ID/body after reopening. Accepted declarations remain inspectable; opening another form leaves them intact. Only the original declaring local author/role exports its unchanged packet.

This is the serial-identity prerequisite for finished-cell integration. Authenticated cell output submission, actual final applicable server-route completion, duplicate serial protection across devices, separate physically counted warehouse acceptance, quarantine and separate Chief QA remain unfinished. No cell stock is posted by this feature. Physical factory/ColorOS acceptance remains a separate gate. Exact tests, browser/native evidence and latest source are recorded in the Storage checkpoint.
